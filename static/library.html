🌿
आमलकी (Āmalakī)

Rasa-Guna-Virya Profile

Rasa: Pancharasa (5 tastes except Lavana)
Guna: Guru, Ruksha
Virya: Sheeta (Cooling)
Vipaka: Madhura

Therapeutic Indications

Prameha, Pandu, Shotha, Kasa, Vatarakta. Primary Rasayana in Charaka Chikitsa Sthana.

Ref: Charaka Samhita, Sutra Sthana 27/102
2

Jadi-Buti: Haritaki

🍂
हरीतकी (Harītakī)

Pharmacological Profile

Rasa: Pancha Rasa (Lavana Varjya)
Guna: Laghu, Ruksha
Virya: Ushna
Vipaka: Madhura

Key Formulations

Abhayarishta, Triphala Churna, Pathyadi Kashaya. Known as "The King of Medicines" in Tibetan & Ayurvedic traditions.

Ref: Bhavaprakasha Nighantu, Haritakyadi Varga
3

Jadi-Buti: Bibhitaki

🌰
बिभीतकी (Bibhītakī)

Rasa-Guna-Virya Profile

Rasa: Kashaya, Madhura, Katu
Guna: Laghu, Ruksha
Virya: Ushna
Vipaka: Madhura

Special Notes

Seeds are narcotic in high doses. Fruit pulp is primary therapeutic part. Excellent for Kapha-Vata disorders, Netra Rogas, and Swasa.

Ref: Dhanvantari Nighantu, Guduchyadi Varga
4

Jadi-Buti: Punarnava

🌱
पुनर्नवा (Punarnavā)

Profile & Action

Rasa: Madhura, Kashaya
Virya: Ushna
Vipaka: Katu

"Punar" = Again, "Nava" = New. The renewer. Primary diuretic and anti-inflammatory in Ayurvedic pharmacopeia.

Indications

Shotha (Edema), Mutrakrichra, Vatarakta, Pandu. Key ingredient in Punarnavadi Kashaya.

Ref: Sharngadhara Samhita, Purva Khanda
5

Jadi-Buti: Guggulu

💧
गुग्गुलु (Guggulu)

Resin Profile

Rasa: Tikta, Katu, Kashaya
Guna: Laghu, Snigdha, Vishada
Virya: Ushna
Vipaka: Katu

Shodhana (Purification)

Must be purified in Triphala decoction or Gomutra before internal use. Raw resin causes adverse effects.

Ref: Rasa Tarangini, Chapter 11
6

Jadi-Buti: Ashwagandha

🐎
अश्वगन्धा (Aśvagandhā)

Rasayana Profile

Rasa: Tikta, Kashaya, Katu
Guna: Laghu, Snigdha
Virya: Ushna
Vipaka: Madhura

Name derives from horse-smell (Ashwa) and strength-giving properties. Primary adaptogen and Balya drug.

Ref: Charaka Samhita, Chikitsa Sthana 1/72
7

Jadi-Buti: Shatavari

🌾
शतावरी (Śatāvarī)

Stree Roga Specialist

Rasa: Madhura, Tikta
Guna: Guru, Snigdha
Virya: Sheeta
Vipaka: Madhura

"She who possesses a hundred husbands." Premier female tonic, galactagogue, and Pitta pacifier.

Ref: Ashtanga Hridayam, Uttara Sthana
8

Jadi-Buti: Tulsi

🍃
तुलसी (Tulasī)

Sacred Herb Profile

Rasa: Katu, Tikta
Guna: Laghu, Ruksha
Virya: Ushna
Vipaka: Katu

Three varieties: Rama (Green), Krishna (Purple), Vana (Wild). All possess Krimighna, Jvaraghna, and Shwasahara properties.

Ref: Bhavaprakasha, Padma Khanda
9

Jadi-Buti: Neem

🌳
निम्ब (Nimba)

Bitter Guardian

Rasa: Tikta, Kashaya
Guna: Laghu, Ruksha
Virya: Sheeta
Vipaka: Katu

Supreme Krimighna and Rakta Shodhaka. Used externally for Kushtha and internally for Pitta-Kapha detoxification.

Ref: Sushruta Samhita, Sutra Sthana 46
10

Jadi-Buti: Brahmi

🧠
ब्राह्मी (Brāhmī)

Medhya Rasayana

Rasa: Tikta, Kashaya
Guna: Laghu
Virya: Sheeta
Vipaka: Madhura

Note: Often confused with Mandukaparni (Centella asiatica). True Brahmi is Bacopa monnieri. Both are Medhya but differ in potency.

Ref: Charaka Samhita, Chikitsa 1/3
11

Jadi-Buti: Shankhapushpi

🌸
शङ्खपुष्पी (Śaṅkhapuṣpī)

Nervine Tonic

Rasa: Kashaya, Tikta, Madhura
Guna: Laghu, Snigdha
Virya: Sheeta
Vipaka: Madhura

Conch-shaped flower gives it the name. Unmatched in Unmada, Apasmara, and Manasika Vikara treatment.

Ref: Madhava Dravya Guna, Page 214
12

Jadi-Buti: Guduchi

🔄
गुडूची (Guḍūcī)

Amrita - The Immortal

Rasa: Tikta, Kashaya, Katu
Guna: Laghu, Snigdha
Virya: Ushna
Vipaka: Madhura

Tridoshahara. One of the few drugs that balances all three doshas simultaneously. Essential in Jvara Chikitsa.

Ref: Charaka Samhita, Chikitsa 3/133

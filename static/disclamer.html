Trust Zone · 04

What this tool is — and isn't

Read this before acting on any match

AyurPedia AI's matching engine is a decision-support and educational tool. It surfaces traditional and research-informed possibilities based on patterns in verified sources. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace the judgment of a licensed physician or registered Ayurvedic practitioner. Always consult a qualified clinician before beginning, changing, or stopping any treatment — herbal or otherwise — especially if you are pregnant, nursing, on medication, or managing a chronic condition.

  • Not for emergencies — call local emergency services for urgent symptoms.
  • Herb–drug interactions can be serious — always disclose all medications to your clinician.
  • Matches are probabilistic guidance, not a definitive diagnosis.
  • Practitioner listings are verified for credentials, not personally endorsed by AyurPedia AI.

Educational tool only — this platform supports, but never replaces, professional clinical judgment.
